# Elias
*A weary custodian of order or memory, often found mending the broken gears of a fading world.*

## At a glance
* **Gender:** Male (99%)
* **Age:** Primarily an enigma; when defined, he is most often **Elderly** (~24%) or **Middle-aged/Adult** (~4%).
* **Role:** Almost exclusively a **Protagonist** (96%).
* **Occupation:** A specialist in precision and preservation. Common roles include **Fixer/Keeper** (14%), **Clockmaker/Horologist/Chronosmith** (8%), **Scavenger** (8%), and **Architect** (4%).
* **Setting:** Frequently oscillates between the cozy/mystical **Oakhaven** (~15%) and the grounded/urban **Seattle** (~9%), or the gothic **Blackwood Manor** (~10%).
* **Genre:** Primarily **Magical Realism** (26%) and **Fantasy** (11%), with significant leans into **Horror** and **Mystery**.

## Personality
Elias is defined by a tension between **precision** and **weariness**. He is overwhelmingly **observant** (36%) and **skilled** (17%), possessing a temperament that is **quiet**, **steady**, and **meticulous**. While he often presents a facade of **logical** or **structured** composure, there is a deep-seated **loneliness** (14%) and **melancholy** (11%) beneath the surface. He is the man who notices the one gear out of alignment or the one shadow that doesn't belong.

## What they want
He seeks to impose or restore **order** against the encroachment of chaos. Whether he is trying to maintain the "structural integrity" of a family, the "resonance of a faded memory," or the "symmetry" of a blueprint, Elias is driven by a need for **stability, meaning, or preservation** in a world that is inherently entropic.

## What haunts them
Elias is a man defined by **regret** and **the weight of the past**. His "wounds" typically involve a fear of the unpredictable, the loss of a loved one, or the heavy burden of "stolen memories." He is often haunted by his own previous attempts to control time or emotion through rigid structures, only to find those structures have become his cage.

## Their usual story
The "Elias Arc" typically follows a transition from **rigidity to release**. He begins as a man obsessed with the measurable, the predictable, and the permanent. Through a catalyst—a mysterious machine, a supernatural entity, or a romantic interest—he is forced to confront the "beautiful chaos" of existence. 

**The Ending:** The tone is highly polarized. 
* **The Hopeful Path (~47%):** He accepts imperfection, finds emotional connection, and finds peace in the ephemeral.
* **The Tragic Path (~21%):** He is consumed by the very things he tried to fix, or is erased by the "cosmic debt" of his own choices.
* **The Bittersweet Path (~23%):** He completes his task but remains a solitary, weary figure, forever changed by the weight of his craft.

## Who they keep company with
He is rarely truly alone, though he often prefers it. His most frequent companion is **Clara** (42%), who typically serves as his emotional anchor or love interest. Other recurring names include **Julian**, **Leo**, and **Maya**, often appearing in his "domestic/family" iterations.

## Variants and outliers
* **The Domestic Elias:** A rare, grounded version (found in "The Miller Household") who is a civil engineer or architect in a contemporary setting, dealing with spreadsheets rather than spellbooks.
* **The Villainous/Antagonist Elias:** An extreme statistical outlier (1 appearance) where he serves as the obstacle rather than the hero.
* **The Translucent/Ghostly Elias:** A horror-variant who is not a man of flesh, but a "thief of memories" or a "shadowy shell" struggling against erasure.

## Field notes
* **The Precision Fetish:** The model has a recursive obsession with "clocks," "architects," and "symmetry" when naming an Elias. This character is the model's go-to avatar for exploring the conflict between human logic and cosmic randomness.
* **The Melancholy Default:** If a prompt asks for a "story" without much direction, the model defaults to a "weary, wise, solitary man" archetype. Elias is the model's "safe" vessel for profound sadness.
* **The Clara Constant:** There is a high correlation between the name Elias and the name Clara; they function as a modular romantic unit within the machine's narrative logic.
