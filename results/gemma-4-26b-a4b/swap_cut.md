# Name swap: Elias's stories, re-continued with other names

20 stories that mention Elias were cut at his first mention and continued with each name. **Elias** is the control (same cut, re-sampled). Markers are counted in the CONTINUATION only, since the text before the cut is shared. If a new name keeps the markers about as often as Elias, the story was set before the name; if they fade, the name was carrying them.
